Hello. I am a first-year DPhil student at the University of Oxford. I work on AI safety and interpretability. My research focuses on how intelligence emerges and evolves in AI models through interaction with the world, and how to ensure these systems remain aligned with and beneficial to humanity. I am fortunate to be supervised by [Fazl Barez](https://fazlbarez.com/) and [Maike Osborne](https://maikeosborne.com/). Before Oxford, I did my undergraduate studies at National Taiwan University (NTU), where I majored in Electrical Engineering with a double major in Economics.
